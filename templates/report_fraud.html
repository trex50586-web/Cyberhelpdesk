🚨 CYBER FRAUD HELP

Report Cyber Fraud

If you have been a victim of cyber fraud, act quickly and report the incident through the official channels.

📞

Financial Cyber Fraud?

For immediate assistance with financial cyber fraud, contact the national cyber fraud helpline.

Call 1930

What Should You Do?

1

Contact Your Bank

If money has been transferred without your permission, contact your bank or payment service immediately.

2

Call 1930

Report financial cyber fraud through the national cyber fraud helpline as soon as possible.

3

File a Cybercrime Complaint

Submit your complaint through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Open Cybercrime Portal →
4

Keep the Evidence

Save screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers, emails, messages and other relevant information.

⚠️ Important

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, password or banking credentials with anyone claiming to help you recover your money.
