Cyber Awareness

Stay informed. Stay safe. Online.

Cyber safety is for everyone. Here you'll find simple and practical tips for kids, young people and senior citizens to stay safe in the digital world.

Kids

Be safe, be smart, be kind online.

1 Keep your personal information private.
2 Don't talk to strangers online.
3 Ask a parent or teacher if something feels wrong.
4 Be careful with links and downloads.
5 Use strong and unique passwords.
6 Think before you post or share.
7 Don't reply to mean or harmful messages.
8 Keep your games and devices updated.
9 Use privacy settings on apps and games.
10 Don't share your location publicly.
11 Be kind and respectful online.
12 Tell a trusted adult about suspicious activity.

Young Adults

Think. Verify. Stay Cyber Safe.

1 Be careful with phishing emails and fake links.
2 Never share OTP, PIN or banking details.
3 Use two-factor authentication (2FA).
4 Check social media privacy settings.
5 Be careful with fake job and investment offers.
6 Check website URLs before entering information.
7 Use strong and unique passwords.
8 Avoid suspicious downloads and unknown apps.
9 Think carefully before sharing personal information.
10 Respect others and avoid cyberbullying.
11 Keep devices and apps updated.
12 Report suspicious online activity.

Senior Citizens

Stay alert. Stay safe. Stay connected.

1 Don't share personal or financial information.
2 Be careful with calls from unknown people.
3 Never share OTP, PIN or passwords.
4 Check messages and links before opening them.
5 Use trusted and secure websites.
6 Keep your phone and apps updated.
7 Avoid clicking suspicious advertisements.
8 Don't make payments to unknown accounts.
9 Ask a trusted family member when unsure.
10 Be careful while using social media.
11 Keep important data backed up.
12 Report cyber fraud quickly through official channels.

🛡️ A safer internet starts with awareness.

Stay informed, share knowledge and help build a safer digital community.
