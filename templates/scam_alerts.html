Live Scam Alert Wall

Learn about common online scams and how to protect yourself.

💳
UPI SCAM

Fake UPI Payment Request

Scammers may send a fake payment request and ask you to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

Example:
"You have received a payment request. Enter your UPI PIN to accept."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
🛒
OLX / MARKETPLACE

Fake Buyer or Seller Scam

A scammer may pretend to be a buyer or seller and send fake payment screenshots or QR codes.

Example:
"Scan this QR code to receive your payment."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Never scan an unknown QR code to receive money.
💼
JOB FRAUD

Fake Online Job Offer

Fraudsters may offer easy work-from-home jobs and demand registration or security fees.

Example:
"Pay ₹999 registration fee to start earning ₹5,000 daily."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Do not pay money to get a job without verification.
📱
OTP SCAM

Fake Customer Support

Scammers may pretend to be bank or company support agents and ask for OTPs or account details.

Example:
"Your account will be blocked. Share the OTP immediately."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Never share OTP, PIN or passwords with anyone.
🎁
PRIZE SCAM

Fake Lottery or Prize

You may receive messages claiming that you won a lottery, gift or prize and asking for a processing fee.

Example:
"Congratulations! Pay ₹2,000 to claim your prize."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Do not pay fees for unexpected prizes.
🔗
PHISHING

Fake Link or Website

Fraudsters can send links that look like genuine banking, shopping or government websites.

Example:
"Your account needs verification. Click this link now."
🛡️ Safety Tip: Check the website address before entering personal details.

🚨 If You Become a Victim

Report financial cyber fraud immediately through the official cybercrime reporting system or call the cyber fraud helpline.
